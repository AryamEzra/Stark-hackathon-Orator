# Orator

AI-powered speaking coach that helps students practice public speaking and articulation through daily voice prompts — and doubles as a spoken-language learning tool.

## What it does

- Every day, get a short-answer or public-speaking topic to respond to out loud.
- Your answer is transcribed and analyzed for both **content** (structure, clarity, did you answer the question) and **delivery** (filler words, pace, pauses).
- Get concrete, specific pointers on how to communicate better — not just a score.
- Practice speaking in a language you're learning the same way: daily spoken prompts, AI feedback, vocabulary support — learning by speaking, not just studying.
- Strong speakers can get verified and paid to coach others through the platform.

## Why

Public speaking and articulation — especially in a second language — are rarely taught directly, even when the underlying ideas are strong. Orator turns speaking into a daily, trainable skill with real feedback instead of guesswork.
